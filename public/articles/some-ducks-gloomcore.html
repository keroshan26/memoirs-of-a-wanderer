Some ducks I happened to come across

Photography

The Pond · April 11, 2025

Photo captured and edited by yours truly 🙂

I recently came across a few videos on TikTok relating to a concept called "gloomcore." From my understanding, it is essentially photos, videos, etc., captured and given a more moody overlay, or if you are lucky, the setting already possesses the desirable traits, and there's no need to use any editing tools to embellish your work. In my case, the day in question was incredibly moody with overcast skies, and I came across two ducks bathing in a small lake, quacking with joy. Suffice to say, the one on the right was none too happy that I had come across them, but I couldn't help but take a shot, but I have an affinity for the darker, more bluer side of "gloomcore," so voila!
