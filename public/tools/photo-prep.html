Photo prep

Gets a photo ready for the Nocturne gallery: shrinks it to 1600px, adds the © keroshangovender.com watermark, compresses it, strips hidden location data, and writes the gallery code for you. Your photo never leaves this device — nothing is uploaded.

1 · Choose a photo

Tap to choose a photo, or drag one here JPG or PNG. Landscape (wide) shots look best in the gallery.

2 · Name it & write the caption

3 · Save the photo

It saves as photo.jpg. Upload that file into public/images/ on GitHub.

4 · Copy the gallery code


    
    
Paste it into public/index.html, just above the line
      <!-- END OF GALLERY PHOTOS --> (or after the last </section> in the Nocturne gallery).
